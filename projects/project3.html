Pre-K Online & Hybrid Expectations

Early Childhood Education | Online Learning | Hybrid Instruction

Project Overview

This project is a slide presentation created for Pre-K students and families to communicate expectations for online and hybrid learning. The slides were embedded in Canvas for families to reference and also presented at the start of live Zoom sessions with students. To support engagement, we incorporated hand gestures as cues throughout the presentation as well.

Audience: Pre-K students, families, and instructional staff.
Tools Used: Google Slides, Canvas LMS, Zoom, and interactive engagement strategies.
