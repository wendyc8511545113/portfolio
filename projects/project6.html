Project Overview

A thoughtful online course helps learners understand where to begin, how to find resources, and what to do next. This collection features video demonstrations of how I created instructional pages in Canvas LMS, making my course-building process visible and explaining it for other educators.

As a teacher, I built Canvas courses for mathematics, science, and social studies. I also developed an onboarding course for new families to complete as they learned to navigate Canvas. These experiences involved creating learning resources for different audiences, from students working through subject content to families becoming familiar with an online learning environment.

The recordings below provide a closer look at my hands-on Canvas experience and how I explain the steps involved in building course pages. Together, they complement my instructional materials by showing both the creation process and my approach to supporting educators.

My Role

I created the Canvas pages demonstrated in these recordings and recorded the walkthroughs to explain how I built them. My work combined teaching experience, curriculum development, and practical use of Canvas to support online learning.

  • Built Canvas courses to support instruction in multiple subjects.
  • Created a Canvas onboarding course to introduce new families to the platform.
  • Demonstrated my page-creation process through recorded tutorials.
