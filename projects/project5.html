Project Overview

This microlearning resource was designed to introduce middle school students to the concept of Vibe Coding — using artificial intelligence as a collaborative partner to transform ideas into technology-based solutions. Developed as a student-facing instructional experience, the lesson guides learners through identifying real-world problems, crafting effective AI prompts, and iterating on their ideas through a design-thinking process.

This resource emphasizes project-based learning, creativity, and problem-solving while making emerging technologies accessible to novice learners. Through guided reflection, scaffolded examples, and actionable challenges, students are encouraged to think like innovators and use AI as a tool for learning, creation, and positive impact.
